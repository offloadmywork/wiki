## About ADRs

Architecture Decision Records document important architectural decisions along with their context and consequences. They help us:

- Remember why we made specific choices
- Understand the context at the time
- Revisit decisions when circumstances change
- Onboard new team members

## Template (ADR)

```markdown
# ADR-NNNN: Decision Title

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD  
**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded

## Context

What is the issue we're facing? What factors are at play?

## Decision

What did we decide to do?

## Consequences

What are the positive and negative outcomes of this decision?

### Positive
- Benefit 1
- Benefit 2

### Negative
- Trade-off 1
- Trade-off 2

### Neutral
- Other impact 1

## Alternatives Considered

What other options did we evaluate?

1. **Alternative 1** - Why not?
2. **Alternative 2** - Why not?
```

## Naming Convention

Use format: `NNNN-short-decision-title.md` (e.g., `0001-use-nodejs-runtime.md`)
