## Guidelines

- **One idea per file** - Use descriptive filenames (kebab-case)
- **Include context** - Why this idea matters, what problem it solves
- **Link related items** - Reference projects, research, or decisions when relevant
- **Date your ideas** - Add creation date in frontmatter or at the top
